Humanoid robots are entering warehouses, yet most companies rent them instead of buying. This animated documentary explains why, using GXO and Agility Robotics as the case study. We cover the Robots as a Service model, why owning a machine that loses value ties up cash, how newer models such as Digit 5 change the math, and what 100,000 totes moved in Georgia show. We also look at why GXO's CEO says production humanoids are about two years away. Subscribe to The World In the Making for more animated documentaries on science, history and business.
