## Claims and sources
- Panamax beam 32.31 m (106 ft) in a 33.5 m (110 ft) chamber, so about 2 ft per side: Panama Canal Authority vessel requirements, Wikipedia "Panamax".
- Original chambers 320 m (1,050 ft) long, 33.5 m wide: Panama Canal Authority, "Design of the locks".
- Canal opened 15 Aug 1914, expansion opened 26 Jun 2016: Panama Canal Authority, Wikipedia "Panama Canal".
- Neopanamax chamber 427 x 55 m, ships to 366 m (1,200 ft) long and 51.25 m beam, so about 6 ft per side: Panama Canal Authority vessel requirements.
- Locks fill by gravity, no pumps; 101,000 cubic meters (about 26 million gallons) per chamber; Gatun: 3 steps, +26 m (85 ft): Panama Canal Authority, Wikipedia "Panama Canal locks".
- 13,951 transits in FY2026, 13,404 in FY2025, 11,240 in FY2024 (down 20.2%): Panama Canal Authority press releases and annual report. One ship every 38 minutes is 13,951 / 365 days.
- About 5% of world trade: Panama Canal Authority statement as repeated on Wikipedia (other sources use other measures).
- 3.5 football fields is 1,050 ft / 300 ft. 40 Olympic pools is 101,000 m3 / 2,500 m3. Eiffel Tower 330 m (1,083 ft).

## Not literal
- The hull drift in the mules scene is exaggerated for the demonstration (the real gap is 4 px on screen).
- The staircase is labelled "not to scale".
- Coastlines on the globe are hand-simplified outlines, not survey data.
- Word times are estimated (faster-whisper could not run), so on-screen cues follow line timing.
